University campuses are busy places where students often lose or misplace their belongings. 
Things like ID cards, keys, water bottles, books, and bags are lost in classrooms, libraries, 
cafeterias, and other campus areas every single day. 
The way campuses currently handle this problem is outdated and unreliable. Students put up 
handwritten notices on bulletin boards, send messages in WhatsApp groups, or just ask around. 
These methods have serious limitations, notices get torn down or buried, WhatsApp messages 
scroll away in minutes, and word-of-mouth only reaches a few people. 
Because of this, a student who lost something has no reliable way to check if someone found it. 
And a student who found something has no easy way to reach the owner. Items end up sitting 
unclaimed for days, or are simply discarded because no connection was made. 
The Campus Lost & Found app solves this directly. It is a shared mobile platform where any 
registered student can post a lost or found item with a photo, description, campus location, and 
contact details. All students can browse and search these posts in real time and contact the poster 
to return the item. When an item is returned, the post can be marked as resolved so the feed stays 
clean.
